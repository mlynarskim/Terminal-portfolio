BMS Monitor: LiFePO4 is an independent application designed to monitor and configure compatible battery management systems through Bluetooth. It is not an official application of any battery management system manufacturer.

2. Battery and BMS Information Processed Locally

The application may process the following information received directly from a connected battery management system:

  • Battery voltage and cell voltages
  • Charging and discharging current
  • Battery power, state of charge, and remaining capacity
  • Temperature measurements
  • Balancing status and balancing current
  • Protection, alarm, charging, and discharging status
  • Battery cycle information
  • Battery management system settings
  • Device model, hardware version, software version, device name, and device serial number
  • Bluetooth signal strength

This information is processed locally on your device to display battery status, create measurement history, estimate remaining operating time, and allow you to review or change supported battery management system settings.

Battery measurements, BMS settings, cell voltages, Bluetooth device data, local measurement history, and exported settings backups are not intentionally transmitted to Google AdMob or used by the Developer for advertising.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain information to provide, limit, personalize where permitted, measure, secure, and improve advertisements. The Developer does not sell battery information or personal information. The Developer does not intentionally provide battery readings, BMS configuration values, Bluetooth device data, local history, or exported settings backups to Google for advertising.

The application uses Google User Messaging Platform to request and store privacy choices where legally required. The consent message and available options may vary by region and by Google requirements.

Users in supported regions can review or change their advertising privacy choices from Settings, then Advertising and privacy, then Manage advertising privacy.

Changing a privacy choice may affect whether advertisements are personalized, limited, or available. A change does not prevent all advertising related processing where processing is permitted or required for purposes such as delivering contextual advertisements, security, fraud prevention, frequency control, or measurement.

6. iOS App Tracking Transparency

On supported iOS versions, the application requests permission through Apple App Tracking Transparency before its first advertising request. The system prompt explains that permission allows advertising identifiers to be used to provide and measure personalized advertisements.

If you deny or restrict permission, the iOS advertising identifier remains unavailable. Denying ATT permission does not block Bluetooth communication, battery monitoring, local history, settings backups, or other BMS features. Advertisements may still be contextual or limited where permitted by your Google User Messaging Platform choices, platform settings, and applicable law.

You can review or change the ATT permission later in the iOS privacy settings. The application and Google advertising services must respect the current platform authorization status.

7. Bluetooth Access

The application requires Bluetooth access to find, connect to, read data from, and send supported commands to compatible battery management systems.

Bluetooth access is used for communication with devices selected by the user. Bluetooth device data and scan results are not intentionally supplied to Google AdMob.

On older Android versions, the operating system may classify Bluetooth scanning as requiring location permission. The application does not use Bluetooth scan results to determine your physical location. Google may separately infer an approximate location from the IP address used when an advertisement request is made.

8. Information Stored Locally

The following information may be stored in the private application storage on your device:

  • Selected application language
  • Measurement history containing timestamps, battery voltage, current, state of charge, and temperature
  • A locally saved copy of battery management system settings
  • Application preferences
  • Advertising consent choices and privacy status used by Google User Messaging Platform

The local settings backup may include the date of the backup, detected protocol version, battery management system model or device name, and supported configuration values.

Battery and BMS information remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it. Advertising privacy choices may be processed by Google services as necessary to apply your selections.

9. Exported Settings Backups

The application allows you to export a settings backup as a JSON file.

The exported file may contain battery management system configuration values, protocol information, the device model or device name, and the date when the backup was created.

You choose where the exported file is saved and whether it is shared with another person or service. Files exported through the operating system are controlled by you and by the applications or storage providers you select.

The Developer does not receive a copy of an exported file, and exported settings backups are not intentionally transmitted to Google AdMob.

10. System Device Backups

Depending on your device settings, locally stored application data may be included in an operating system backup, device transfer, iCloud backup, or Google backup.

Such backups are provided and controlled by Apple, Google, the device manufacturer, or another storage provider selected by you. Their processing of backup data is governed by their own privacy policies.
