1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BaggageAR Measure, referred to as the App, handles information when you use it.

BaggageAR Measure helps users estimate baggage dimensions and compare them with baggage limits published by airlines.

2. Privacy Summary

BaggageAR Measure does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data through the App.

  • Camera images are processed directly on your device.
  • Camera images, scans, and measurements are not uploaded to our servers.
  • The App does not require an account.
  • The App does not contain advertising services.
  • The App does not use tracking or analytics services.
  • The App does not collect precise location information.
  • The App does not access your contacts.
  • The Developer does not receive your payment card information.

3. Camera and Augmented Reality Processing

The App requires access to your device camera to provide baggage scanning and measurement features.

Depending on your device and the selected scanning method, the App may process live camera frames, LiDAR depth information, detected surfaces and planes, the shape and position of objects, estimated dimensions, and information needed to place virtual measurement guides.

This information is processed directly on your device. It is not transmitted to the Developer or stored on an external server.

The App does not take or save photographs. Camera frames are used temporarily to provide scanning and augmented reality features.

You may disable camera access at any time in your device settings. Scanning features will not operate without camera access.

4. Information Stored Locally

The App may store limited information locally on your device, including:

  • App preferences.
  • The selected airline and baggage category.
  • Manually entered or measured baggage dimensions.
  • Information required to restore the current App state.
  • Premium access status.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer.

You can remove locally stored information by deleting the App. Purchase records maintained by Apple are associated with your Apple Account and may remain available for purchase restoration.

5. Purchases

The App may offer an optional one time Premium purchase through Apple’s App Store.

Purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. The Developer does not receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or Apple Account password.

The App may receive limited information from Apple that is necessary to verify whether a purchase is valid and whether Premium access should be available. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policies and legal terms.

6. Airline Information and External Links

The App contains baggage information associated with airlines, including dimensions, baggage categories, and allowance notes.

This information is provided for general informational purposes. Airline policies may change and may depend on the fare, route, travel class, aircraft, airport, operating carrier, and other conditions.

The App may provide references or links to airline websites. If you open an external website, that website may process information under its own privacy policy. The Developer does not control external websites or their privacy practices.

7. No Advertising, Analytics, or Tracking

The App does not contain advertising software and does not track users across apps or websites.

The App does not use third party analytics, does not create advertising profiles, and does not sell personal information.
