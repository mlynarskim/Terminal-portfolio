Privacy Policy

UltraNotch is developed by Mateusz Młynarski. This policy explains what the app processes on your Mac, which optional features contact other services, and how to control or remove your information.

Privacy contact and controller for support correspondence and this website: Mateusz Młynarski, an individual developer, mlynarski.mateusz@gmail.com. The developer does not operate a cloud service for your panel content.

The short version

No UltraNotch account is required. The app contains no developer analytics SDK, advertising SDK, data broker integration or automatic crash upload service. Camera, calendar, clipboard, file content and layouts are handled locally by UltraNotch. Optional YouTube, weather, Spotify artwork and App Store services make network requests to their providers. Local processing does not mean that every feature is offline or that external providers process no data.

2. Permissions and optional access

Camera: macOS permission enables the local mirror. Calendar: macOS full event access is required to read the agenda, although UltraNotch uses it only to read. Automation: separate approval lets the app read and control the selected Apple Music or Spotify player. Files: access comes from your file selection, paste or drag and drop, with bookmarks for later access.

Accessibility: the optional two Option key gesture observes modifier changes and the states of the two Option keys. Standard app shortcuts can also match their specific key and modifier combination. UltraNotch does not record typed text or maintain a keystroke history. The app does not request device location, Contacts, Health, microphone or screen recording access for these features. System permission is a technical access control; it is not a blanket authorization for unrelated processing.

You can disconnect features in the app and revoke protected access in System Settings. Disabling a feature may make it unavailable, but does not require disabling the rest of the app. Launch at Login uses Apple’s ServiceManagement framework and can be turned off.

3. Optional external services and recipients

YouTube and Google

UltraNotch uses the YouTube embedded player, an external YouTube service, inside a WebKit view. Loading a video sends its identifier and connection information to Google and the player’s supporting services. Depending on the player, device, choices and content, Google may process your IP address, browser or device information, playback interactions, diagnostic information, advertising related information and cookies or similar session identifiers. Ads and third party player requests can be present. This is not an UltraNotch analytics service, but it is external processing.

The native app uses a nonpersistent WebKit data store and the youtube-nocookie.com embed host. It does not request Google account authorization or your Google password. These measures do not guarantee zero cookies, no collection, no tracking by an external service or deletion of Google’s server records. The provider describes its privacy enhanced mode for website embeds; UltraNotch does not promise identical guarantees for a native app web view.

The saved video link remains in local preferences until you change or remove it. Closing the notch stops the player; web views that are hidden or too small are paused. Removing a web view or quitting ends its temporary app session, but does not erase provider records. You can avoid the connection by not loading videos. Read Google’s Privacy Policy, YouTube’s Terms of Service and YouTube’s embedding guidance. Google privacy and deletion controls apply to information held by Google.

Apple geocoding and MET Norway weather

Searching for a city sends the text you enter to Apple’s geocoding service. The app does not read your current device location. Choosing a city saves its name and coordinates rounded to two decimal places and sends those coordinates directly to MET Norway’s Locationforecast service. Requests also disclose your IP address, app name and version, and the developer’s support contact in the User Agent. They do not include your support email, calendar, clipboard, file content or music history.

MET Norway documents that it logs client IP addresses and requested coordinates in Oslo, Norway. Local caching reduces repeat requests. The selected city and saved forecast remain on your Mac until replaced or cleared; forecasts can refresh while the configured weather feature is in use. A selected city can be a city other than where you are. See Apple’s Privacy Policy, MET Norway’s terms and logging explanation, and its privacy information. Forecast data is attributed to MET Norway under CC BY 4.0; displaying or formatting it does not imply their endorsement.

Spotify and Apple Music

Player control uses local macOS Automation. Apple Music artwork is read from the local player. For Spotify, an artwork URL returned by the player can be requested directly from an allowed Spotify image host over HTTPS. That server receives your IP address and the requested artwork resource. The app’s artwork session disables cookies and uses a memory cache without its own disk cache. The provider may retain its server records. UltraNotch does not transmit a listening history to the developer or request a Spotify OAuth account. Your installed players have their own network activity, account settings and Spotify or Apple policies.

Apple App Store and StoreKit

Apple handles billing, account authentication, payments, refunds and purchase restoration. StoreKit supplies signed product and transaction information that the app verifies locally to determine Lifetime access, including product and transaction identifiers and refund or revocation status. UltraNotch does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password, and does not send purchase records to a developer backend. Apple may hold transaction records under its own obligations. See App Store and Privacy.

Links, Siri, Terminal and support

A saved link opens in your browser and discloses the normal browser request to the destination. Suggested links, including GitHub, ChatGPT and Claude, are not contacted merely because they appear in the library. There is no direct OpenAI or Anthropic API integration and no developer AI training pipeline in this build. Siri is opened as a system app; voice or typed requests you give to Siri follow Apple’s settings and policies. Terminal is opened for your chosen folder. No Terminal command is automatically executed.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, attachments and any details you choose to send. The support address uses Gmail, provided by Google. A Copy App Details button copies the app version, macOS version and Free or Lifetime state to your clipboard; it does not automatically email or upload them. Do not send passwords, full payment details or unnecessary personal information. External apps can separately sync or upload content under your own settings; UltraNotch does not control that behavior.

4. Purposes and legal grounds

Where the GDPR or similar law applies to processing for which the developer is responsible, the purposes and grounds are:

  • Providing requested functionality, responding to a customer’s support request and handling purchase access: performance of a contract or steps requested before entering a contract, where Article 6(1)(b) applies.
  • Responding to general inquiries, maintaining necessary support records, handling legal claims, and delivering and securing the website: legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f). These interests are customer service, reliable delivery, security and protecting legal rights, balanced against your rights.
  • Records that law requires us to maintain: legal obligation under Article 6(1)(c).
  • Optional processing or storage that legally requires consent: Article 6(1)(a) and applicable electronic communications rules. Such consent can be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You do not have to provide an email address to use the local app. We need contact information to reply to a support message. Providing a city or loading a video is optional; without it the corresponding online feature cannot be supplied. External providers also describe their own purposes and grounds in their policies. An app permission does not authorize advertising profiling or unrelated marketing.

5. Tracking, advertising and identifiers

UltraNotch does not contain a developer advertising network, analytics SDK, attribution SDK, advertising identifier access, data broker integration, cross app profiling system or automatic developer telemetry upload. The developer does not sell local panel content or use it to train an AI model. The app does not add its own advertisements.

YouTube may deliver its own advertising and collect player related information. IP addresses and temporary identifiers can be personal data even when a name is not supplied. External player processing is described in section 3 and the provider’s privacy policy. The absence of an UltraNotch tracking SDK does not mean the player is offline or that its provider cannot collect or combine information.

There is no automated decision making or profiling by UltraNotch that produces legal or similarly significant effects. A forecast, timer or control animation is not such a decision.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local preferences and saved content: remain until changed, removed or the relevant app data is deleted. Local data is not remotely accessible to the developer, so we cannot delete it from your Mac for you.
  • Clipboard: session memory only; clear it, disable monitoring or quit. Pausing is not deletion and does not clear the system clipboard.
  • Camera: live capture only; no UltraNotch frame archive. Stop the mirror or revoke access.
  • Agenda: displayed entries are held in memory; Disconnect clears the agenda. This does not delete the events in your calendars.
  • Files: remove each shelf item to remove its bookmark or managed copy. Exported copies and original files are separate and remain under your control. Backups or a cloud folder can retain their own copies.
  • Weather: changing or removing the city clears the corresponding app forecast cache. Provider logs are not under the developer’s control.
  • Wallpapers: generated images are stored locally and the cache is trimmed. Restore the original wallpaper before deleting wallpaper app storage. Restoring or resetting settings is not a promise that every cached image is erased.
  • Support: ordinary correspondence is retained for the time needed to resolve your request and genuine follow up, then deleted when no longer needed for those purposes. If a dispute or legal record obligation applies, relevant records may be kept for the applicable limitation or mandatory retention period. You can request deletion; necessary retained records are restricted to that purpose.
  • Provider information: Apple, Google, Spotify and MET Norway apply their own retention and deletion rules. Ending a local session does not delete their records. Use their privacy controls or contact them for deletion they control.

A factory preferences reset preserves purchase entitlement and selected productivity content; it is not an “erase all personal data” function. Uninstalling a macOS app may leave its container or support files. For complete local removal, first restore your wallpaper, remove saved content and revoke access, quit the app, and remove the app’s own container and support data. Contact support for instructions rather than deleting unrelated system files. Apple manages App Store purchase records separately.

7. Security and international processing

The native app runs in Apple’s App Sandbox, uses user selected file access and system permission controls, and uses HTTPS for its own remote requests. Local preferences and managed files are not protected by a separate UltraNotch password or an app specific encryption vault. Their protection depends on macOS account access, storage settings such as FileVault, and your backup choices. No software or network can be guaranteed completely secure. macOS may maintain local crash reports or share diagnostics with Apple under your separate system settings; UltraNotch does not run its own automatic crash upload service.

The developer does not operate a cloud database for app content. External providers, including Google, Apple, Spotify, the email provider, GitHub and Cloudflare, may process information outside your country and, where applicable, outside the EEA. Their current privacy notices identify the responsible entities and applicable transfer safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where they apply. MET Norway states that its API access logs are stored in Norway. We do not claim that all provider processing takes place within the EU or that every provider has the same retention period.

10. This website and demonstration

The first party website source uses local HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG illustrations and the app icon. It has no analytics script, advertising pixel, external font, developer account, newsletter form or first party cookie or local storage tracker. Demo states live in page memory and reset on reload. Music and weather are fictional; the sample shelf does not read or upload visitor files. Color and volume sliders affect only the illustration.

An actual YouTube iframe is inserted only after the visitor chooses to load it and confirms the external service notice. The player can process connection, device, viewing and advertising information and use cookies or similar technologies. You can keep the illustration and use the rest of the page without it. Switching the demo view or collapsing the notch removes that iframe. The browser or provider may retain information from the session.

Website infrastructure: our public website is served through GitHub Pages and its delivery infrastructure, with Cloudflare providing authoritative DNS for the domain. GitHub documents security logging of visitors’ IP addresses. Page delivery can also process requested URLs, timestamps, HTTP headers and browser information. Cloudflare DNS handles domain queries and related technical metadata; DNS service alone is not the same as receiving every page URL or running an analytics script. JDM is also used for associated domain or hosting account administration. Domain administration does not itself mean that every page request passes through that provider’s web server. These infrastructure services do not provide UltraNotch with your app’s local clipboard, calendar, camera or files.

Infrastructure records are used for service delivery, operation, security, abuse prevention and legal obligations. Provider retention follows those purposes and the applicable service terms; we do not promise a fixed number of days that the providers have not specified for this configuration. We do not build a separate visitor profile database from these requests. See GitHub Pages and IP logging, GitHub’s Privacy Statement, Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and JDM’s privacy and service documents. If the delivery configuration changes, this notice will be updated. External links and email links open their respective services.
