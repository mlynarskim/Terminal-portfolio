Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Calmie ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Calmie mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Analytics and Device Information

App versions that include analytics use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, to understand feature usage and improve Calmie. Analytics may record app launches, the selected session type, duration ranges, whether a session started or finished, use of the sound mixer and statistics screens, and the platform and device category, such as iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro, or Mac where supported. The service also processes an app installation identifier, device model, operating system and app version. Google may derive an approximate region from network information. Calmie does not send your name, mood, Apple Health data, or detailed personal session records as analytics events.

Analytics information is used for product analytics. It is not used for advertising or cross app tracking. Analytics data is not combined by Calmie with your name or an account because Calmie does not require an account.

Meditation Preferences

Meditation settings such as session duration, selected sounds, and user preferences may be stored locally on your device to personalize your experience.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App.
  • Improve functionality and user experience using product analytics.
  • Fix bugs and technical issues.
  • Store personalized meditation preferences locally.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited analytics and device information is shared with Google Analytics for Firebase as described above. We do not share data for advertising or cross app tracking.

4. Third Party Services

Versions of Calmie that include analytics send the information described above to Google Analytics for Firebase. Google processes this information to provide analytics services under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Calmie does not use analytics for advertising or cross app tracking and does not sell analytics data.

5. Children's Privacy

Calmie is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You may contact us with questions about your privacy or requests concerning analytics data. Google may retain information already received according to the provider's retention settings. Aggregated reports may not be attributable to a single app installation.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
