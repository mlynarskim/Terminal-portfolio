4. System requirements and feature limits

UltraNotch requires macOS 15 or later. It is a native macOS utility, not an iPhone app. Some features require installed apps, compatible hardware, a network connection, system permissions or specific macOS versions.

  • Music controls require a supported Apple Music or Spotify player and Automation approval. Their own memberships or content charges are separate from Lifetime.
  • System volume can be changed only if the selected output device supports it. Screen Dimmer is a visual layer, not hardware backlight control. The app does not claim to control keyboard backlight.
  • Wallpaper hue creates a local variant of a static image. Dynamic wallpapers keep their system provider and can use a separate tint or dimming overlay; UltraNotch does not control their animation timeline.
  • Camera Mirror is a local preview, not a recording or conferencing service. Calendar access is used to read the agenda, not edit your events.
  • Clipboard history is optional and held in memory. Exclusion and sensitive content detection cannot recognize every confidential item. Turn it off before copying information you do not want read.
  • File Shelf is a local convenience feature, not a backup, archive or cloud transfer service. Removing a bookmark does not delete an original file; removing a managed copy does not remove copies you exported elsewhere. Keep your own backups.
  • Terminal opens your chosen folder and copies saved command text. You decide what to paste or execute. Siri availability and its processing are controlled by Apple and your system settings.
  • Measured Prism Fold motion requires a compatible publicly exposed hinge sensor. Other Macs use lid state and wake events. macOS may sleep or switch off the display before the animation completes. The feature does not prevent sleep.
  • Effects and materials adapt to hardware, OS support and accessibility settings. Liquid Glass support depends on the system; older supported versions use alternative native materials.
