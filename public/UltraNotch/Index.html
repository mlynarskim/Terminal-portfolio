A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.

Ask us something else ↗
Does my Mac need a physical notch?

No. The panel sits at the top of your display. UltraNotch requires macOS 15 or later and supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Some controls depend on your hardware, connected displays and permissions.

Can I remove the music tile?

Yes. Every tile is optional. Choose a preset, remove what you do not use, and arrange your own panel. You also choose which tiles appear in Mini.

Does video keep playing when the notch closes?

No. Closing the notch stops YouTube playback. You can display video in a sufficiently large Mini or Max view. The standard YouTube player and its embedding rules still apply.

Can I change my macOS wallpaper colors?

UltraNotch can create a recolored version of a static wallpaper without altering its original file. Dynamic wallpapers keep their system animation and support a separate tint or dimming overlay. It does not control a dynamic wallpaper’s timeline.

Which quick controls can I use?

System volume, player volume, screen dimmer, static wallpaper hue, wallpaper dimmer, wallpaper tint color and tint amount. Edge sliders and corner dials are part of Lifetime. Audio support depends on your output device. The website demonstrates three of these controls.

What is included in Lifetime?

Lifetime is a single feature unlock, not a subscription. It adds separate Home and Work layouts, six tiles per panel, edge and corner controls, Surface Lab and automatic Prism Fold. Compatibility and third party service availability can change. See our Terms of Use.

Is this page showing the real app?

The interactive preview is a browser simulation using fictional music, sample files and sample weather. It does not access your Mac. You can optionally load an actual YouTube embed. The native app runs locally on macOS.
