1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bms Monitor: Lifepo4 processes information when you use the mobile application.

The application is provided by Mateusz Młynarski, referred to in this Privacy Policy as the Developer, we, us, or our.

Contact email: mlynarski.mateusz@gmail.com

Bms Monitor: Lifepo4 is an independent application designed to monitor and configure compatible battery management systems through Bluetooth. It is not an official application of any battery management system manufacturer.

2. Information Processed by the Application

The application may process the following information received directly from a connected battery management system:

  • Battery voltage
  • Cell voltages
  • Charging and discharging current
  • Battery power
  • State of charge
  • Remaining battery capacity
  • Temperature measurements
  • Balancing status and balancing current
  • Protection and alarm information
  • Charging and discharging status
  • Battery cycle information
  • Battery management system settings
  • Device model
  • Hardware version
  • Software version
  • Device name
  • Device serial number
  • Bluetooth signal strength

This information is processed locally on your device to display battery status, create measurement history, estimate remaining operating time, and allow you to review or change supported battery management system settings.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The application does not require a user account.

The Developer does not collect, receive, store, or transmit your battery readings, battery settings, Bluetooth device information, location, advertising identifiers, contacts, photos, messages, or other personal information.

The application does not use advertising services, behavioral tracking, analytics services, or tracking technologies.

The application does not sell or share personal information.

The application does not send battery readings or settings to an external server operated by the Developer.

4. Bluetooth Access

The application requires Bluetooth access to find, connect to, read data from, and send supported commands to compatible battery management systems.

Bluetooth access is used only for communication with devices selected by the user.

On older Android versions, the operating system may classify Bluetooth scanning as requiring location permission. The application does not use Bluetooth scan results to determine your physical location and does not collect, store, or transmit precise or approximate location information.

5. Information Stored Locally

The following information may be stored in the private application storage on your device:

  • Selected application language
  • Measurement history containing timestamps, battery voltage, current, state of charge, and temperature
  • A locally saved copy of battery management system settings
  • Application preferences

The local settings backup may include the date of the backup, detected protocol version, battery management system model or device name, and supported configuration values.

This information remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

6. Exported Settings Backups

The application allows you to export a settings backup as a JSON file.

The exported file may contain battery management system configuration values, protocol information, the device model or device name, and the date when the backup was created.

You choose where the exported file is saved and whether it is shared with another person or service. Files exported through the operating system are controlled by you and by the applications or storage providers you select.

The Developer does not receive a copy of an exported file.

7. System Device Backups

Depending on your device settings, locally stored application data may be included in an operating system backup, device transfer, iCloud backup, or Google backup.

Such backups are provided and controlled by Apple, Google, the device manufacturer, or another storage provider selected by you. Their processing of backup data is governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

The application does not automatically share information with the Developer or with third parties.

Information may leave your device only when you deliberately export a settings file, share a file, create a device backup, or use an operating system feature that transfers application data.

9. Data Retention

Measurement history and local settings backups remain stored on your device until you delete them, clear the application data, or uninstall the application.

Temporary Bluetooth information and live battery readings are kept in memory only for as long as necessary to provide the application features, unless a reading is added to the local measurement history.

The Developer does not operate a server that retains application data.

10. Deleting Your Data

You may delete measurement history using the available application controls.

You may remove all locally stored application information by clearing the application data in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Exported files must be deleted separately from the location where you saved them.

The Developer cannot remotely access or delete local information because the application does not send that information to the Developer.

11. Data Security

The application relies on the security protections provided by the mobile operating system and private application storage.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device, exported backup files, device backups, and access to the connected battery management system.

You should not share exported settings files if they contain information you consider confidential.

12. Children

The application is not designed to collect personal information from children.

The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or external data collection, the Developer does not receive information about the age or identity of users.

Battery configuration should only be performed by a competent adult who understands the relevant electrical and battery safety requirements.
